ERI Field Research

A research institute built for interconnected challenges

EED Research Institute is a multidisciplinary research and development organization founded in 2023 with a strong commitment to advancing scientific inquiry, innovation, and evidence-based solutions for today's most pressing global challenges. The Institute focuses on key thematic areas including Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH), energy, climate change, and agriculture, recognizing the deep interconnection between these sectors and their impact on sustainable development.

Through research, collaboration, capacity building, and practical implementation, EED Research Institute seeks to generate knowledge, inform policy, and support communities, institutions, and development partners in creating resilient, inclusive, and sustainable solutions.
