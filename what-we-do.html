Our Work

What We Do

Advancing evidence-based solutions across WASH, energy, climate change, and agriculture.

Four research pillars

Our work is organised around four thematic areas. They are deliberately interconnected — water security is an energy question, energy access is a climate question, and both determine whether agriculture is viable.

01

WASH

Water security, safely managed sanitation and hygiene behaviour — and the governance arrangements that decide whether services still work years after they are built.

Explore →
02

Energy

Clean cooking transitions, energy access and mini-grids, energy and climate finance, and the productive use of energy in enterprise.

Explore →
03

Climate

Adaptation and resilience in water, energy and food systems, climate finance, carbon markets and removals, and the alignment of climate with development policy.

Explore →
04

Agriculture

Smallholder productivity under climate risk, irrigation and water for agriculture, land use, and the food systems and value chains that turn yield into income.

Explore →
